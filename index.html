OneHand rclone

OneHand rclone is a personal OAuth integration used with the open-source tool rclone to manage files in the owner's own Google Drive.

  • It uses the Google Drive API.
  • It is used only by its owner. It is not a service offered to third parties, and no one else can sign up for it.
  • It only accesses the owner's own Google Drive data, after the owner authorizes it with Google OAuth.
